Add Kitchen Hub to your Home Screen
  1. Open your browser's Share or menu.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. On iPhone or iPad, if you see Open as Web App, turn it off.
  4. If you want a particular browser on iPhone or iPad, make it your default browser first.
  5. Tap Add.
Open Kitchen Hub

The shortcut opens Kitchen Hub in your default browser, so it can use that browser's existing Google sign-in.
